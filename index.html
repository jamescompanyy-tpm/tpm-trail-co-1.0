Founder · Trail Guide

Your guide

James
Rivera

James Rivera has spent years exploring the forests, summits, and hidden corners of New England's trail system. What started as a personal practice became a calling — to share the restorative power of wild places with anyone willing to lace up their boots.

TPM Trail Co. — The People's Medicine — is built on the belief that nature heals the mind like medicine. Every guided hike is an invitation to slow down, look closer, and remember what the land has always known.

Wilderness First Responder certified. Follows Leave No Trace principals. Lifelong student of the trail.

— James
